CloudTech Solutions - Customer Support FAQ
=============================================

BILLING AND ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT
---------------------------------

Q: How do I upgrade my subscription plan?
A: To upgrade your subscription, navigate to Settings > Billing > Change Plan. Select your desired plan and confirm. The upgrade takes effect immediately, and you'll be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your current billing cycle. If upgrading from Free to Pro, you'll also need to add a payment method first. Enterprise upgrades require contacting our sales team at enterprise@cloudtech.io who will generate a custom quote based on your organization's size, compliance requirements (SOC2, HIPAA, FedRAMP), and expected usage patterns including API call volume, storage needs, and number of seats.

Q: Can I get a refund if I downgrade mid-cycle?
A: We do not offer prorated refunds for mid-cycle downgrades. When you downgrade, your current plan features remain active until the end of your billing cycle. The lower rate applies starting with your next billing cycle. Exception: If you downgrade within 48 hours of an accidental upgrade, contact support for a full refund. For annual plans, downgrades take effect at the next renewal date. If you need to downgrade due to financial hardship, our retention team can offer a 3-month discount at 40% off while you evaluate your needs. Contact billing-help@cloudtech.io with subject line "Hardship Review" and include your account ID.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover credit/debit cards. For Enterprise plans, we also accept wire transfers and ACH payments. Prepaid cards are accepted but may require additional verification. Cryptocurrency payments are not currently supported. All payments are processed in USD; currency conversion fees are determined by your bank. For invoiced billing (Enterprise only), NET-30 and NET-60 terms are available with approved credit. Purchase orders are accepted for annual commitments exceeding $50,000.

Q: How do I update my billing information?
A: Go to Settings > Billing > Payment Methods. Click "Edit" next to your current payment method or "Add New" to add a different one. For security, we require re-authentication when changing payment details. If your card is expired, update it within 7 days to avoid service interruption. For Enterprise accounts with invoiced billing, contact your dedicated account manager or email ar@cloudtech.io to update billing contacts or payment instructions. Note: changing from card to invoice billing requires a plan review and minimum annual commitment of $24,000.

Q: Why was my payment declined?
A: Common reasons for declined payments include: insufficient funds, expired card, incorrect billing address, bank fraud prevention blocks, or international transaction restrictions. Try updating your card details, contacting your bank to authorize the charge from "CLOUDTECH*SUBSCRIPTION", or using an alternative payment method. If issues persist after 3 attempts, your account will be placed on a 14-day grace period during which all features remain active. After the grace period, your account is suspended (read-only access to data, no API calls). After 30 additional days of non-payment, data deletion begins per our retention policy.

TECHNICAL SUPPORT
------------------

Q: How do I reset my API keys without causing downtime?
A: Resetting API keys requires careful coordination to avoid service interruption. Follow this exact process: Step 1 - Generate a new key by navigating to Settings > API Keys and clicking "Create New Key" (do NOT click "Regenerate" which invalidates immediately). Step 2 - Update all applications, services, and CI/CD pipelines to use the new key. Verify each service is functioning with the new key by checking the API key usage dashboard (Settings > API Keys > Usage) for requests from the new key. Step 3 - Once all traffic has migrated (typically wait 24 hours to catch cron jobs and batch processes), delete the old key. Important: The old key remains valid until explicitly deleted, giving you a safe migration window. For production environments with multiple services, we recommend using our key rotation SDK which handles blue-green deployment of keys automatically. Install with `pip install cloudtech-keyrotate` and configure with your service manifest YAML.

Q: What are the API rate limits?
A: Rate limits depend on your plan and are enforced per API key:
- Free: 100 requests/minute, 1,000 requests/day, 10 concurrent connections
- Pro: 1,000 requests/minute, 50,000 requests/day, 100 concurrent connections
- Enterprise: 10,000 requests/minute, unlimited daily, 1,000 concurrent connections
Rate limit headers are included in every response: X-RateLimit-Limit (your cap), X-RateLimit-Remaining (requests left this window), X-RateLimit-Reset (Unix timestamp when window resets). When rate limited, you receive HTTP 429 with a Retry-After header. Implement exponential backoff with jitter: wait = min(base_delay * 2^attempt + random(0, 1000ms), max_delay). The base_delay should be 1 second and max_delay 32 seconds. Burst allowance: all plans allow 2x the per-minute rate for bursts under 5 seconds. If you consistently hit limits, contact us about dedicated rate limit pools for Enterprise customers.

Q: How do I configure SSO/SAML for my organization?
A: SSO configuration requires an Enterprise plan and admin access. Complete setup process: Step 1 - Navigate to Organization Settings > Security > SSO Configuration. Step 2 - Select your Identity Provider from the dropdown (supported: Okta, Azure AD/Entra ID, OneLogin, Google Workspace, PingFederate, or Custom SAML 2.0). Step 3 - Enter your IdP metadata URL or upload the metadata XML file. For Okta, this is found under Applications > Your App > Sign On > Metadata URL. For Azure AD, navigate to Enterprise Applications > Your App > Single Sign-On > Federation Metadata XML. Step 4 - Configure attribute mapping: email (required, maps to user principal), firstName, lastName, groups (for role-based access). The groups attribute should send group names matching your CloudTech team names for automatic role assignment. Step 5 - Test the connection by clicking "Test SSO" which authenticates a single user through the full SAML flow and reports any attribute mapping issues. Step 6 - Enable SSO enforcement for all organization members. Warning: Once enforced, password-based login is disabled for ALL non-admin users. Keep at minimum 2 admin accounts with password access for emergency recovery (called "break-glass accounts"). Setup typically takes 15-30 minutes for standard IdPs and up to 2 hours for custom SAML configurations requiring claim transformations.

Q: My webhook deliveries are failing. How do I troubleshoot?
A: Webhook failures have several common causes. Check this diagnostic sequence: Issue 1 - Timeout: Your endpoint must return HTTP 200-299 within 30 seconds. If processing takes longer, return 202 Accepted immediately and process asynchronously. Issue 2 - SSL errors: Your endpoint must have a valid, non-expired, non-self-signed certificate from a trusted CA. Let's Encrypt certificates work fine. Check with: `curl -vI https://your-endpoint.com 2>&1 | grep "SSL certificate"`. Issue 3 - Content type: Your server must accept POST requests with Content-Type: application/json. Some frameworks (like Flask) require explicit JSON parsing configuration. Issue 4 - IP allowlisting: If you firewall inbound traffic, allow our webhook IP ranges: 52.14.0.0/16, 18.220.0.0/16, 3.134.0.0/16 (us-east), 52.59.0.0/16, 18.196.0.0/16 (eu-west). These ranges are published at api.cloudtech.io/v1/meta/ip-ranges and updated quarterly. Issue 5 - Payload validation: Verify the webhook signature using the X-CloudTech-Signature header (HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body with your webhook secret). Retry behavior: Failed webhooks retry 5 times with exponential backoff (1min, 5min, 30min, 2hr, 12hr). After all retries exhaust, the webhook is disabled and you receive an email notification. Re-enable in Settings > Webhooks after fixing the underlying issue. View detailed delivery logs including request/response headers and bodies in Settings > Webhooks > Recent Deliveries (retained for 7 days).

Q: How do I migrate data between environments (staging to production)?
A: Use our CLI tool for environment migration. Install: `npm install -g @cloudtech/cli` or `brew install cloudtech-cli`. Authentication: Run `cloudtech auth login` and complete the browser-based OAuth flow. Export: `cloudtech export --env staging --format json --include-refs > staging_export.json` (the --include-refs flag preserves relationships between objects). Preview: `cloudtech import --env production --file staging_export.json --dry-run` shows exactly what will be created/updated/skipped without making changes. Execute: `cloudtech import --env production --file staging_export.json --confirm` performs the actual migration. Important details: IDs are regenerated to avoid conflicts (a mapping file is written to ./migration_mappings.json for reference). Object references are resolved using the mapping file automatically. Large exports (>1GB or >100,000 objects) should use streaming mode: `cloudtech export --env staging --stream --output-dir ./export_chunks/` which produces batched files to avoid memory issues. Environment variables (secrets, API keys) are NOT included in exports for security - use `cloudtech env sync --from staging --to production --keys KEY1,KEY2` to selectively copy specific variables. Always verify the migration by running `cloudtech verify --env production --baseline staging_export.json` which reports any discrepancies.

DATA AND PRIVACY
-----------------

Q: Where is my data stored and can I choose my region?
A: Data residency depends on your account region selected at signup:
- US accounts: Primary in AWS us-east-1 (Virginia), failover in us-west-2 (Oregon)
- EU accounts: Primary in AWS eu-west-1 (Ireland), failover in eu-central-1 (Frankfurt)
- APAC accounts: Primary in AWS ap-southeast-1 (Singapore), failover in ap-northeast-1 (Tokyo)
Backups are stored in the failover region (same geographic area, never cross-continental). Enterprise customers can request single-region storage for strict compliance (data never leaves the primary region, but this disables cross-region disaster recovery). Region migration is possible but requires a maintenance window of 2-4 hours during which your account is read-only. Request region migration through Settings > Account > Data Residency or contact support. Data sovereignty certification documents (confirming storage locations) are available for download in Settings > Compliance > Certificates for audit purposes.

Q: How long do you retain my data after account deletion?
A: Upon account deletion request (Settings > Account > Delete Account, requires admin + email confirmation): Active data (documents, configurations, user records) is removed from primary storage within 24 hours and becomes immediately inaccessible. Backup systems containing your data are purged within 30 days as backup rotation cycles complete. Audit logs (login events, API access records, admin actions) are retained for 90 days after deletion as required by our SOC2 compliance obligations and financial regulations - these are anonymized (user IDs replaced with hashes) but retained for potential audit response. Anonymized, aggregated usage analytics (feature usage counts, not individual behavior) are retained indefinitely for product improvement and are not attributable to your account. Before deletion, export your data: Settings > Account > Export Data generates a complete archive (JSON format) including all content, configurations, integrations settings, team memberships, and audit history. The export link is valid for 72 hours. Deletion is irreversible after 7 days - during the first 7 days you can contact support to cancel the deletion and restore your account.

Q: Is my data encrypted and what encryption standards do you use?
A: Yes, comprehensive encryption is applied at multiple layers: At rest: AES-256 encryption using AWS KMS. Free and Pro plans use AWS-managed keys (aws/s3, aws/ebs, aws/rds). Enterprise customers get dedicated customer-managed KMS keys (CMK) with configurable automatic rotation (90 days default, minimum 7 days). In transit: TLS 1.3 for all external API communications. TLS 1.2 minimum for internal service-to-service calls (1.3 where supported). Certificate pinning available for mobile SDK integrations. In processing: Sensitive fields (API keys, OAuth tokens, webhook secrets, user credentials) use application-level envelope encryption with field-level access controls - even our database administrators cannot read these values without the application-layer decryption key. Backup encryption: Encrypted with keys separate from primary storage keys, ensuring a primary key compromise does not expose backup data. Key hierarchy: Master key (HSM-stored) > Data encryption key (per-tenant) > Field encryption key (per-sensitive-field). Enterprise BYOK (Bring Your Own Key): Upload your own KMS key ARN during onboarding. You retain key custody and can revoke access at any time (this immediately renders your data unreadable - use with caution). Key deletion has a mandatory 7-day waiting period during which we'll attempt to contact you to confirm intent.

INTEGRATIONS
-------------

Q: How do I connect my Slack workspace for notifications?
A: To connect Slack: Step 1 - Go to Settings > Integrations > Slack. Step 2 - Click "Add to Slack" which redirects to Slack's OAuth consent screen. Authorize the CloudTech app for your workspace. Required scopes: chat:write, channels:read, groups:read (for private channels), users:read (for @-mentions). Step 3 - Select notification channels: Choose which Slack channels receive which event types. You can configure per-project channels (e.g., #proj-alpha-deploys) or a single catchall channel. Step 4 - Configure notification preferences: Options include all events, errors only, deployment events only, or custom rules using our filter syntax (e.g., `event.severity >= "warning" AND event.source IN ["api", "worker"]`). Step 5 - Test by clicking "Send Test Message" which posts a sample notification to your configured channels. The integration supports interactive messages: deployment approval buttons (approve/reject directly from Slack), incident acknowledgment, and workflow triggers. Slash commands are also available after installation: `/cloudtech status` shows system health, `/cloudtech deploy <env>` triggers a deployment pipeline, `/cloudtech oncall` shows the current on-call rotation. Bot user token is stored encrypted and can be revoked from Settings > Integrations > Slack > Revoke at any time.

Q: Which CI/CD platforms integrate natively and how do I set them up?
A: Native integrations are available for: GitHub Actions (use `cloudtech/deploy-action@v3` in your workflow YAML, configure with repository secrets CLOUDTECH_API_KEY and CLOUDTECH_PROJECT_ID), GitLab CI (use our Docker image `cloudtech/ci-runner:latest` as a service, configure variables in Settings > CI/CD > Variables), CircleCI (orb available: `cloudtech/deploy@2.1`, add to your .circleci/config.yml), Jenkins (plugin: "CloudTech Deployment" from the Jenkins plugin marketplace, version 4.2+), Bitbucket Pipelines (pipe: `cloudtech/deploy-pipe:1.0`), and Azure DevOps (extension: "CloudTech Deploy Tasks" from the VS Marketplace). All integrations support: automatic deployment on merge to specified branches, environment promotion (staging -> production), rollback triggers on health check failures, deployment annotations in our dashboard for correlation with metrics, and Slack/Teams notifications at each stage. For platforms without native integration, use our CLI in any script: `cloudtech deploy --env production --version $GIT_SHA --wait` returns exit code 0 on success, 1 on failure (for CI pass/fail). The --wait flag blocks until the deployment is healthy (configurable timeout, default 5 minutes).
